Docya

A simple appointment book for doctors. Set your working hours, book patients into open 30-minute slots, get a reminder before each visit, and keep private notes. Patients do not need the app.

Support

Questions, problems or a request to delete data? Email gentlyawesome@gmail.com and include the email address you signed up with. We reply within a few working days.

You can also delete your account and all of its data yourself in the app: Profile > Delete account.

Common questions

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?". We email you a code to choose a new one.

Do my patients see anything? No. Patients are not users; you type in their name and optional phone number, and only you can see them.

I do not get reminders. Open Settings > Notifications > Docya on your iPhone and allow notifications, then check the reminder time on the Profile tab.
