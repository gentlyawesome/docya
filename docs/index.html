What Docya does

Everything a small practice needs. Nothing it doesn't.

One doctor, one phone, one calm list of who's coming in next.

01

Book a patient in seconds.

Type a name and an optional phone number, tap a day, pick an open slot. Docya only offers times inside your working hours and crosses out the ones already taken, so a double booking can't happen.

  • Calendar with open days marked
  • 30-minute slots in your time zone
  • Private notes on every appointment
02

Your hours, your rules.

Set a working window for each day of the week. Taking Friday off? Flip a switch. Docya turns your hours into bookable slots in your own time zone, and never offers a time you're not working.

  • Weekly working-hours editor
  • Turn a day on or off in one tap
  • Times follow your phone's 12 or 24-hour clock

Common questions

Do my patients see anything?
No. Patients aren't users. You type their name and optional phone number, and only you can see them.

I can't book, there are no time slots.
Add your working hours in the Schedule tab. New accounts start with Monday to Friday, 9 to 5.

I don't get reminders.
Allow notifications for Docya in your iPhone's Settings, then check the reminder time in the Profile tab.
