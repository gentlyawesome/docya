The problem

A forgotten appointment costs you twice

Once for the empty chair. Again for the patient who wanted that time and could not get it.

  1. The chair sits empty

    A patient forgets, the slot passes, and the time you set aside earns nothing. Nobody else could take it because nobody knew it was free.

  2. Calling to confirm takes time you do not have

    You are treating someone, so the confirmation call never happens.

  3. Your phone calendar tells only you

    The patient has nothing in their pocket telling them they have a visit.

What changes

A diary that speaks up for itself

Reminders go out on their own

You book the visit and Docya texts the patient. The plan: one reminder 24 hours before and one 2 hours before.

See who confirmed

The plan is for patients to reply "C" to confirm, so you know who is coming before the day starts and can fill a slot you know will be empty.

Know what no-shows cost you

Docya will count missed visits each month. One hematology clinic pilot saw no-shows fall from 38.6% to 25.5% with text reminders. Your result will depend on your clinic.

FAQ

Before you join

Something we have not covered? Email gentlyawesome@gmail.com.

Do my patients need to download anything?

No. They get a text message on their regular phone number. They do not need an account.

How are my patients' details protected?

Each doctor can see only their own appointments, and you can delete your account from the app. Read the privacy policy before you add patient names. Docya has not had a legal review for medical data rules in your country, so enter only a name and a phone number.

What does the pilot cost?

Nothing for 30 days for the first 10 clinics. After that you choose a plan or go back to the free Diary.

What if my patients do not reply?

Reminders still go out. The confirm reply is optional, and you see who did not answer.

Can I use it on Android?

Not yet. Docya is on iPhone only.

Support

Help for Docya users

Questions, problems or a request to delete data? Email gentlyawesome@gmail.com and include the email address you signed up with. We reply within a few working days.

You can also delete your account and all of its data yourself in the app: Profile > Delete account.

Read the user guide

I cannot book a patient, or there are no time slots

You need working hours first. Open the Schedule tab and add the days and hours you see patients. New accounts start with Monday to Friday, 9 to 5.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. In the app open Profile > Export my data. It gives you a text copy of your profile, working hours and appointments to save or send to yourself.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?". We email you a code to choose a new one.

Do my patients see anything?

Not yet. Patients are not users today. You type in their name and optional phone number, and only you can see them.

I do not get reminders

Open Settings > Notifications > Docya on your iPhone and allow notifications, then check the reminder time on the Profile tab.
