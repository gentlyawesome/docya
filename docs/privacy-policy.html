Privacy Policy for Docya

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Docya lets patients request appointments with doctors, and lets doctors confirm them. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

2. What We Collect

You create an account with an email address and password. We store on our servers:

  • Account and profile: email, first and last name, role (patient or doctor), and optional details you add such as phone number, date of birth, and gender
  • Doctors only: specialization, license number, clinic name, consultation fee, bio, time zone, and weekly availability
  • Appointments: doctor, patient, date and time, status (pending, confirmed, cancelled, completed), and the optional reason or notes you enter

Appointment reasons and notes may contain health information you choose to share. Please share only what you are comfortable with.

3. Who Can See Your Data

  • Patients see their own profile and appointments, and the public details of doctors.
  • A doctor sees appointments booked with them and the profile of patients who booked with them.
  • Other patients can only see that a time slot is taken, never who booked it.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

4. Stored on Your Device

  • Your sign-in session, so you stay signed in
  • A cache of the doctor list, your favourite doctors, and appointment reminder settings

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; they are not sent through our servers.

5. What We Don't Collect

  • No payment information
  • No location or GPS data
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking

6. Service Providers

Accounts, database, and authentication are provided by Supabase, which processes and stores data on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted by row-level security rules.

7. Your Rights and Control

  • Edit: update your profile any time from the Profile tab
  • Cancel: cancel appointments from My Bookings
  • Delete: use "Delete account" in the Profile tab to permanently delete your account, profile, and appointments. You can also email us to request deletion.

8. Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile and appointments are removed from our systems, except for routine backups that expire on their normal schedule.

9. Children's Privacy

Docya is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, notify you in the app. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

11. International Users

Your data is processed on servers that may be located outside your country. By using Docya you consent to this transfer.
