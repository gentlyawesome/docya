    Privacy Policy for Docya

    Last updated: October 4, 2026

    1. Introduction

    Docya is an appointment book for doctors. Doctors create an account, set their working hours, and record appointments for their patients. Patients do not use the app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

    2. What We Collect

    A doctor creates an account with an email address and password. We store on our servers:

    • Doctor account and profile: email, first and last name, optional phone number, specialization and time zone
    • Working hours: the weekly schedule the doctor sets
    • Appointments: date and time, status (confirmed, cancelled, completed), and the patient's name and optional phone number, exactly as the doctor typed them, plus any private notes the doctor adds

    Notes may contain health information. Doctors should record only what they are permitted and need to keep.

    3. Patient Information

    Patients do not have accounts and do not use Docya. A patient's name and phone number are entered by their doctor. The doctor is responsible for having a lawful basis to record and keep that information and for honouring the patient's rights over it (for example access and deletion requests). Docya stores it on the doctor's behalf and shows it only to that doctor.

    4. Who Can See Your Data

    • A doctor sees only their own profile, working hours, and appointments. Other doctors cannot see them.
    • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

    Your sign-in session is stored on your device so you stay signed in. The app also remembers on your device whether you have seen the welcome cards and which of the getting-started steps you have finished; this stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. If you use appointment reminders, the app also keeps your reminder choices and the list of reminders it scheduled on your device, and asks iOS to show you a notification before each appointment. Reminders are created and shown on your device only; they are not sent through our servers. By default a reminder shows the time and date but not the patient's name; you can choose to include the name, in which case it may be visible on your lock screen.

    5. What We Don't Collect

    • No payment information
    • No location or GPS data
    • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking

    6. Service Providers

    We use your email address only to run your account: to send the code that confirms your sign-up and the code that resets a forgotten password. Those emails are sent through an email delivery provider acting on our behalf. Accounts, database, and authentication are provided by Supabase, which processes and stores data on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted by row-level security rules.

    7. Your Rights and Control

    • Edit: update your profile and working hours any time from the app
    • Cancel: cancel an appointment from the Appointments list
    • Export: use "Export my data" in the Profile tab to get a copy of your profile, working hours and appointments
    • Delete: use "Delete account" in the Profile tab to permanently delete your account, working hours, and every appointment (including the patient names and numbers in them). A patient who wants their details removed should ask their doctor, or email us and we will pass the request on.

    8. Retention

    We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, working hours and appointments are removed from our systems, except for routine backups that expire on their normal schedule.

    9. Children's Privacy

    Docya is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

    10. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, notify you in the app. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

    11. International Users

    Docya is available worldwide. Our database and sign-in service are hosted by Supabase in the Asia-Pacific region (Seoul, South Korea), so your data, and your patients' details that you enter, are processed and stored outside your own country if you live elsewhere. By using Docya you consent to this transfer. Doctors are responsible for making sure that recording their patients' details in Docya is permitted where they practise.
