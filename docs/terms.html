    2. Who may use Docya

    Docya is for licensed doctors and clinicians, such as physicians and dentists licensed by the Professional Regulation Commission, who are at least 18 years old and legally allowed to practise where they work. You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details secret and for everything done with your account.

    3. What Docya is, and is not

    Docya helps you keep a list of appointments, working hours, reminders and private notes. It is a scheduling tool only. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, is not a medical record system, and is not a medical device. Do not rely on it as your only record of a patient's care, and do not use it for emergencies.

For your patients' details you are the personal information controller; Docya handles them on your behalf.
    • Do not enter anything you are not permitted to store outside your own systems.

    5. Patient text reminders

    • Text reminders are a pilot. They are available only if we switch them on for your account, and we may switch them off at any time.
    • You may ask Docya to text a patient only if that patient told you they agree to receive the text, and the number you enter is one they gave you for that purpose. Keep your own record of their agreement.
    • Reminders are for appointments only. You must not use them for advertising, promotions or any other message. The text is fixed and you cannot change it.
    • Texts go only to Philippine mobile numbers, between 7:00 am and 8:00 pm in your time zone, within daily and monthly limits. Texts can be delayed or fail because of the phone networks, so a reminder is a convenience, not a promise that a patient will be told. You stay responsible for contacting patients when it matters.
