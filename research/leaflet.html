Docya

An appointment book for doctors and dentists, on your iPhone.

Fewer missed appointments, without a single call.

Patients forget. I'm building a way for the diary to remind them for you, and I'd like your honest opinion before I go further.

  • 1An appointment book that fits in your pocketSet your hours, book a patient in seconds. Live on the App Store today.
  • 2A text reminder to the patientSent automatically before the visit, only to patients who agreed to it. Being tried with the first clinics.
  • 3Your patients' details stay yoursOnly you can see them. You can export or delete everything in the app.
What the patient receives (one text per appointment)
Reminder: appointment with Dr. [Name] on Wed, Oct 14 at 10:30 AM. To change it, call [clinic phone].
It names the doctor, the day and the time. It never says what the visit is for, and it is never advertising.
Free for 30 days for the first 10 clinics. Nothing to pay and nothing to sign. I'll show you how it works in 10 minutes, and you tell me what is wrong with it.
